THE EXPERIMENT

Connecting AI
and human support.

This demo combines a website chat interface, Gemini responses, and human handoff. It is a personal project exploring the integration flow.

HOW THE DEMO WORKS

  1. A customer sends a message through the chat widget.
  2. An n8n workflow routes the request to Gemini.
  3. Supabase broadcasts the response to the chat interface.
  4. When a human is needed, the conversation moves to Telegram with its context.
  5. AI responses pause while the human agent replies.
